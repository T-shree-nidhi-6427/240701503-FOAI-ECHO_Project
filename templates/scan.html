How ECHO reads a memory

  1. Optical Character Recognition
    Tesseract reads the visible text on your device.
  2. Local ML classifier
    Decides what it is: warranty, food, document, subscription, receipt, medicine or other.
  3. Intelligent information extraction
    Finds names, dates, durations and quantities.
  4. Rule-based temporal reasoning
    Works out the date that matters and when to remind you. It never invents missing facts.
